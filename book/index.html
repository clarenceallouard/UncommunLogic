Bring these three things

  1. One process. The one that annoys you most, not the one that sounds most strategic.
  2. Rough numbers. How often it happens, how long it takes, how many people touch it. Rough is fine.
  3. Your tool list. What you already pay for. Half the answers live in a licence you already own.

What happens in the hour

We listen first. Then we ask the questions that make the problem costable. Then we talk through what a solution could look like and what it would not cover. We do not present anything, and we will not send you a proposal you did not ask for.

What happens after

Within forty-eight hours you get one document: the return, every assumption behind it, and the price, set at between one week and one month of that return. Read the method if you want the detail before the call.

What it costs

The hour costs nothing and carries no obligation. If the problem does not need us we will say so on the call.
