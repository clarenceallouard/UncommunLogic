The brief

Nothing gets built against a conversation. We write down what the system must do in testable terms, what it explicitly will not do, what we need from you and by when, and what done looks like. You sign it or you stop there and keep the diagnosis.

The costing

What goes into
the return, and
what does not.

Counted

  • Hours recovered, at the loaded cost of the person currently doing the work.
  • Rework: what it costs when the manual version goes wrong, times how often it goes wrong.
  • Revenue that currently leaks. Unquoted enquiries, unchased invoices, no-shows.
  • Licences and subscriptions the new system makes redundant.
  • Our build cost and any running cost the system carries, subtracted.

Not counted

  • Soft benefits. Morale, agility, being seen as modern. Real, but not bankable, so they are not in the number.
  • Growth we cannot attribute. If we cannot trace the dollar to the system, it does not go in.
  • Your time saved on tasks you would not have done anyway.

Every assumption is listed by name in the document, with the figure we used and where it came from. If you disagree with one, change it and the number moves in front of you.

The pricing rule

One week to
one month of
the return.

Whatever the system returns per month, our price sits between one week and one month of it. Small, obvious jobs sit at the bottom of that band. Systems that take real design sit at the top.

The consequence is arithmetic, not marketing: your payback lands between five and twenty working days. After that the system is returning money against a cost you have already paid.

The handover

What lands
in your hands.

01

The running system

Live, in your accounts, in your tenant, under your billing. Not on our infrastructure.

02

The code

Where code exists, you get the source, the repository and the right to change it. No obfuscation, no licence key.

03

The instructions

How it works, how to change the parts you will want to change, what to do when it stops, and what it costs to run.

04

The walkthrough

A session with the people who will actually use it, recorded so the next hire can watch it.

No hostage clause

We do not hold credentials, we do not keep the system on our servers, and there is no retainer required to keep it alive. If you never speak to us again the thing keeps working. That is the point of handing over the code.

The refusal

Four things
we will not
do.

  1. Build something that does not pay for itself inside a month. If the costing says no, the answer is no, and you keep the costing.
  2. Move your customer data somewhere you have not agreed to. Where the data sits is a decision we put in front of you in writing, before the build, not a default we pick for you.
  3. Build something only we can run. If it needs us standing next to it, it is not finished.
  4. Take a job we cannot deliver in the window we quoted. Two people can only build so much at once. When we are full we say we are full and give you a date.

Questions we get

Asked and
answered.

What does the first hour cost?

Nothing. If the problem does not need us, we say so, and there is no invoice.

What if the return is not there?

Then there is no proposal. You keep the costing document and the map of where your time goes, and you have spent an hour.

Is seven days real?

For everything in the catalogue, yes, counted from the day you sign the brief. A build spanning several systems, several teams or a data migration is not a seven-day job. When that is the case it is stated in the forty-eight hour document, with its own timeline, before you commit.

Do we own it?

Yes, on final payment. The running system, the source code where code exists, the credentials and the documentation. See terms of use for what we retain, which is our own pre-existing tools and know-how, and nothing of yours.

Do you resell software?

No. We hold no reseller agreements and take no margin on any tool we recommend, so a recommendation costs us nothing to reverse.

What happens to our data during the build?

We take the minimum needed to build and test, we tell you what that is, and we delete our working copies at handover. Read how we handle information.

Who actually does the work?

Both founders. No account manager, no subcontractor, no offshore build team. See who we are.
