The offer, as a tree

Seventy-eight
things we
build.

One trunk, eleven branches, seventy-eight leaves. Open a branch and its leaves are the things we build there, smallest first.

Every leaf is sized in two-hour build blocks and carries an hours-back estimate. Those estimates are ours, made before we have seen your business. Nothing here is a price: the price is set after the return is calculated, on your numbers.
