72 things we build

The full list, without scripting.

Microsoft 365 & Copilot 14

  • The handover between two teams, fixed · ~30 h/mo · 12 blocks
    The point where work moves between offices, sites or an offshore team is where it gets lost. We map it, then automate the handoff and the status everyone chases.
  • Documents generated from a form · ~26 h/mo · 7 blocks
    Engagement letters, contracts, proposals, quotes. Fill a form, get the finished document with the right clauses and the right numbers. Built on templates you already use.
  • An agent that answers staff questions from your own files · ~24 h/mo · 6 blocks
    Policies, procedures, pricing, past projects. Staff ask in Teams and get the answer with the source, instead of interrupting whoever knows.
  • A model tuned to one job, on your data · ~24 h/mo · 13 blocks
    When the off-the-shelf tools are not accurate enough for the task that matters most, we build the endpoint and keep the data in your Azure region.
  • Invoices and forms read automatically · ~22 h/mo · 6 blocks
    Supplier invoices, timesheets, application forms. Data pulled out, checked, and pushed into the system instead of retyped.
  • A CRM with no new licences · ~20 h/mo · 8 blocks
    Client records, pipeline and follow-ups running on Microsoft Lists or Dataverse, inside the tenant you already pay for. Before you sign anything with Salesforce.
  • A shared inbox that sorts and drafts itself · ~18 h/mo · 5 blocks
    The info@ address that everyone half-watches. Triaged, assigned, and answered from drafts in your house style.
  • Meetings that turn into tasks by themselves · ~16 h/mo · 4 blocks
    Teams recording in, decisions and actions out, assigned in Planner with dates. The follow-up nobody had time to write.
  • The weekly report nobody has to build · ~16 h/mo · 6 blocks
    The numbers someone currently rebuilds every Monday, pulled and published automatically.
  • Approvals that stop living in email · ~14 h/mo · 4 blocks
    Leave, expense, purchase orders, discounts. Requested in Teams, routed to the right person, logged, chased if ignored.
  • Onboarding that runs itself · ~14 h/mo · 5 blocks
    New client or new starter: accounts, folders, checklists, welcome pack, reminders. One trigger, the whole sequence.
  • Copilot licences people actually use · ~12 h/mo · 2 blocks
    You are paying for seats. We audit who has one, find the three tasks per team where it pays for itself, and run the session that makes it stick.
  • SharePoint tidied so AI can actually read it · ~10 h/mo · 7 blocks
    Nothing works until the files are structured and the permissions are right. Unglamorous, and the reason most Copilot rollouts disappoint.
  • What staff can and cannot put into AI · ~4 h/mo · 3 blocks
    A three-tier rule for your data — open, internal, restricted — written on one page, with the technical controls to back it up. Ten minutes to read, saves a notifiable breach.

Getting started with AI 6

  • One assistant for the whole team · ~8 h/mo · 3 blocks
    Same answers, same tone, whoever's on the keyboard. Plus you can finally see who's using what.
  • Talk instead of type · ~6 h/mo · 2 blocks
    Dictate a rambling voice note on the drive home; get back a tidy email, quote or job record.
  • An assistant that knows your business · ~5 h/mo · 1 block
    ChatGPT or Claude set up with your services, prices, policies and tone — so it stops answering like a stranger.
  • An hour, sitting next to you · ~3 h/mo · 1 block
    We use your real work, not a demo, until it clicks. Most people's "I get it" moment happens here.
  • Your five-prompt cheat sheet · ~2 h/mo · 1 block
    One page, written for your job, with the handful of asks you'll actually use every week. Fridge, not folder.
  • Ground rules for what not to paste · ~0 h/mo · 1 block
    A short list of what should never go into a chatbot — customer details, bank data, anything covered by privacy law. Ten minutes that saves you a very bad week.
