How we choose

Four questions,
in this order.

  1. What are you already paying for? An unused licence is the cheapest capability in the building. We look there first.
  2. Where is the data allowed to sit? This is answered before the tool is chosen, not after. It rules options out, and that is the point.
  3. Who has to operate it on a bad Tuesday? The best system your team will not use is worth nothing. We build for the person who will actually be holding it.
  4. What happens when we are gone? If it cannot be maintained by someone who is not us, it is the wrong build.
