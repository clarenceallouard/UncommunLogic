01 / The position

We price after
we prove the
return.

Most firms quote a day rate and hope the value shows up later. We do it in the other order.

You give us one hour and the numbers behind one process. We take forty-eight hours to work out what fixing it is worth to you, in dollars and in hours. Then we set our price at between one week and one month of that figure, and we show you the arithmetic that got us there.

If the return is not there, there is no proposal. That happens, and it is the cheapest outcome available to you.

03 / What we do

Two things. Both
of them ours.

Advisory

Diagnosis, framing, decision support

We map where the time actually goes, quantify it, and tell you which three things are worth automating and in what order. You get the map whether or not you build with us.

Build

Agents, integrations, process automation

We build inside the tools you already pay for, in your tenant and your accounts. Seven days from a signed brief to a system your team is using.

Handover

You get all of it

The running system, the source code where code exists, the credentials, the written instructions and the operating manual. No retainer required to keep it alive, no licence held over you.

Refusal

What we will not build

Anything that does not pay for itself inside a month. Anything that needs your customer data to leave the country without you knowing. Anything that only works while we are standing next to it.
