Why us

Five reasons,
all of them
checkable.

01

The costing comes first

We calculate what fixing your process is worth before we name a price, and we show the arithmetic. If the return is not there we say so and there is no proposal.

02

No margin on anything

No reseller agreements, no partner tiers, no referral fees. A recommendation costs us nothing to reverse, so it is worth what it says.

03

You keep the code

Source, credentials, documentation, all of it, in your accounts. If you never speak to us again the system keeps running.

04

Four things we will not build, published in advance. A firm that never says no is selling capacity, not judgement.

05

06

English and French

Both founders work in both languages, which matters more than it sounds when a European head office is involved in an Australian decision.

The name

Yes, it is
spelled that
way.

Uncommun. The O of “uncommon” was taken out and replaced with a U. The O did not disappear: it became the ring of our mark.

That mark is a NOT gate, the inversion operator of combinational logic, and the ring is its inversion bubble. So the symbol holds the letter the word is missing. It started as a typing mistake between the two of us and we kept it on purpose.

Spelling it right would be common.
