1. We set no cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for preferences, not for “essential functionality”. There is nothing to consent to and there is no banner, because a banner would be theatre.

2. The one thing we store on your device

NameTypeWhat it doesLifetime
ul-introSession storageRecords that the opening animation has already played, so it plays once per visit rather than on every page.Until you close the tab

It holds the single character 1. It is not an identifier, it cannot be read by any other site, it never reaches a server, and removing it only means the animation plays again.

3. Fonts, images and scripts

Everything the site loads comes from this domain. The typefaces are served from our own server rather than from a font service, so opening a page does not tell a third party that you were here. There is no content delivery network, no tag manager and no embedded social widget.

4. What we deliberately do not run

  • No Google Analytics or any other analytics product.
  • No advertising or remarketing tags, no conversion pixels.
  • No session recording or heatmapping.
  • No fingerprinting, and no attempt to identify you across sites.
  • No third-party form or chat service.

The trade-off is that we cannot tell you how many people read a page. We accept that.

5. The one exception: the booking page

The booking page can embed a Google Calendar appointment schedule. If that embed is active and you interact with it, Google can set its own cookies in your browser and will receive whatever you type into it, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We do not control that and we cannot switch it off from our side.

If you would rather not load it, email or call instead. Both are on the contact page and both reach us faster.

6. Blocking any of this

Your browser can block cookies and site data per site, and any mainstream content blocker will stop the booking embed. Nothing on this site breaks if you do, apart from the embed itself.

7. If this changes

If we ever add analytics, we will say so here, name the tool, name what it collects, and change the date at the top of this page before it goes live. We will not add it quietly.
